# 2026-09-06 — Phase I exits: a deployed multi-source MVP

The exit criterion — live site, real postings from at least four sources across two tiers, working
filters, every card crediting its source, no Tier D connector enabled — was met on 2026-08-28 and
verified against the deployed instance rather than a local run:

- 995 vacancies from four sources: Greenhouse 229 and Lever 37 (Tier A), Arbeitnow 629 and Jobicy
  100 (Tier B).
- Filters answer on the deployed API: `keyword=engineer` → 336, `location=Berlin` → 116. A keyword
  of a bare `%` returns 225 rather than all 995, so the `ILIKE` escaping holds in production.
- Every row carries its source name, home URL and `attributionRequired`.
- Ingest returns `401` with no token and with a wrong one; Himalayas (disabled, not cleared) is
  skipped with its reason in the report; no Tier D row exists in the deployed database.

Arbeitnow replaced Himalayas in the four MVP adapters (EM-53): Himalayas' terms require written
approval it has not given, and Arbeitnow was already spiked and cleared.

Found after deploying and fixed inside the phase: `RawPostings` appended a row per fetch (EM-58),
about four days of Neon's 0.5 GB once scheduled hourly — it now keeps one row per posting; and
seniority is mapped from the fields Jobicy and Arbeitnow return (EM-59), 388 of 1,730 rows.

Moved to Phase III: EM-19 (Ashby adapter — no Ashby board found that hires from Georgia) and EM-63
(`hiring_geo` — Arbeitnow carries no eligibility field, 0 of 650).

The phase merged into `main` through PR #16 on 2026-09-06 and was tagged `phase-1`.
