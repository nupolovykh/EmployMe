# 2026-08-22 — hh.ru falsified; the plan is rebuilt as Revision 2

Revision 1 of the plan was built around one source, hh.ru's public API. Its availability was
marked done without anyone having seen a response body. It failed on two independent grounds:

- the public vacancy search has returned `403` to unauthorised callers since April 2026, and
  DDoS-Guard blocked this project's egress address outright (confirmed 2026-08-25);
- the developer agreement forbids passing retrieved data to third-party services, so a publicly
  deployed aggregator was never shippable — a legal ground no technical workaround touches.

Three weeks of planned work went with it: EM-13 (hh.ru client) and EM-14 (manual hh.ru ingest)
were cancelled, and EM-9 became the post-mortem. jobs.ge / headhunter.ge, a scraped second source
in Revision 1 with no terms verdict, was dropped at the same time.

**What changed because of it.** Revision 2 rebuilt the plan around employer ATS boards (Tier A)
and public remote-job APIs (Tier B), with at least four connectors so that no single source can
block a phase. The process rules now in `PROCESS.md` — evidence over assertion, the spike gate,
assumptions with expiry dates, N≥3, sources as rows, a nightly contract test — all come from this
incident. The falsified claim is kept whole as `ASSUMPTIONS.md` A-000; the disqualification is in
`SOURCES.md`.
